# Code of Conduct

AnswerFold follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent, and focus on what's best for the project and the people in it. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately through the email address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so repeatedly.
