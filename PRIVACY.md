# Privacy Policy

**AnswerFold collects no data.** It has no analytics, no tracking, and makes no network requests.

## What it stores

To remember which answers you folded, AnswerFold saves, in your browser only (`browser.storage.local`):

- the path of each chat page where you folded something (for example `/app/abc123`), and
- a short hash of the question each folded answer replies to (not the question itself).

This data never leaves your computer. It's removed when you unfold everything in a chat, or when you uninstall the extension.

## Permissions

- **Access to supported chat sites** (currently `gemini.google.com`): to add the fold bars to the page.
- **Storage**: to remember folded answers, as described above.
