## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-19

### Added

- Fold bar on every Gemini answer: click to fold the answer to a one-line preview, click again to open it.
- Round button to collapse or expand all answers.
- Keyboard shortcut <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd> to collapse or expand all answers.
- Folded answers are remembered per chat, across reloads.
- Styling that follows Gemini's font and light/dark theme.
